Analytics

Everything the platform records, in one place: who's training, what they're completing, how the AI practice is scoring, and what people think of the training itself.

Internal only, and deliberately not linked from anywhere in the platform. It names individual trainees and shows what they wrote and said, and it is still reachable by anyone who has the URL — treat the link as sensitive rather than assuming it's private.
· ·

Who's training

Every trainee by name, and how far through their path they are. The strip is their whole path end to end — one block per academy, sized by how many modules it holds — so a full strip is a finished path. Everyone does Academies 1–4; leadership roles add Academy 5, which is why their strip has an extra block and a bigger denominator.

Activity over time

Every recorded event — module completions, quizzes, practice reps and ratings — by week.

How people train

Device and visit length, recorded per visit. Durations are active time — the clock only runs while someone is on the page and using it, so a tab left open over a break isn't counted as training.

Phone, tablet or desktop

Whether the phone-first, voice-first design matches how it actually gets used.

How long a visit lasts

A visit spans several modules when they're done in one sitting, so this is a sitting, not a page view.

When people train

Every recorded action by day and time of day — before shift, after shift, or on the clock. Built from all activity, so it covers the whole history rather than only since visit tracking started.

By academy

Where people are getting to, and how each academy is landing.

Where people stop

Completions per module. A cliff between two bars is where an academy is losing people.

AI practice

Score distribution

4 is the intended everyday score. A pile-up at 5, or a long tail at 1–2, means the grader needs recalibrating.

Spoken vs typed

Whether the voice-first design is actually being used.

Hardest prompts

Lowest average score, minimum 2 attempts. A consistently low prompt usually means the module didn't teach it clearly — not that people are failing.

AI token use

Every call to the AI and what it consumed — grading a practice answer, and answering an "Ask the Founder" question. Logged by the edge function rather than the browser, so these are actual reported counts. Billing is metered in the Anthropic console; what this answers is where the tokens went.

Where the tokens go

Which feature is consuming them, and how it splits between what we send and what comes back.

Tokens per week

Usage follows activity — a jump here without a jump in practice reps is worth a look.

Tokens per trainee

What one person's training has consumed so far. This is the number that scales with headcount.
